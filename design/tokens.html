Design tokens

Paper theatre after midnight. A small stage of cut-paper layers. Matte clay people. At night the only light is a phone. The interface is warm paper and ink, like a theatre programme. One signal colour means “a notification just arrived”.

Colour

Twelve colours from the brief. No purple or blue-violet anywhere. No gradients except a flat two-stop sky behind a scene. Ping is used for three things only: the primary button, the notification dot and the focus ring.

Speaker colours

These come from the character table. Sarah’s sweater is #2E7D7A. Her name chip uses one step darker (#27706D) so paper text on it passes AA.

Text pairings, checked against WCAG 2.2 AA

Type

Display: Young Serif for the wordmark, headings and scene titles. Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. Body is 20px by default so the A- control can step down to the 18px floor and never below. Captions are 24px by default (22 to 26). Lines stay at 62 characters or fewer.

Self-check against the “fail the design if” list

Fails ifWhat the pack does instead
Purple, indigo or blue-violet gradient, or gradient textNo purple anywhere. The only gradients are four flat two-stop skies behind scenes.
Glassmorphism, frosted cards, glowing bordersOpaque paper with a hard ink edge. Phone glow is drawn as flat stepped shapes.
Sparkle, star, magic-wand, robot, brain or chip imageryNone. Marks are a paper scrap, a signpost, a pencil and a curtain.
A floating assistant bubble or avatarNone. The reader is a quiet step: “Reading your answer...”.
Emoji used as iconsIcons are drawn as SVG.
Three equal feature cards in a row, or any bento gridFour paper tabs at staggered heights and angles, each a different shape of mark.
Inter, Roboto, Poppins, Space Grotesk or system-default lookYoung Serif and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next.
Stock illustration or lorem textAll art is drawn for this story. All text is from the brief.
Everything a pill, everything rounded4px by default. Only chat bubbles are heavily rounded.
Copy that sounds like a launch pageShort plain sentences from the brief. No banned words.
