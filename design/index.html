Where I chose, because the brief was open

  • Primary button label is ink, not paper. Paper on persimmon is 3.3:1, which fails AA. Ink on persimmon is 4.5:1.
  • The wordmark’s persimmon dot is on the second “i” (the one before “Tales”). The first “i” keeps its ink dot so the word still reads.
  • Body text is 20px by default. That lets A- step down to the 18px floor and never below it.
  • Sarah’s name chip is #27706D, one step darker than her sweater, so paper text on it passes AA.
  • Focus ring gets a paper halo on paper-deep surfaces, where plain ping would be 2.9:1.
  • Torn edges are on exactly three things: the Home diorama, the ending title card and the Why notebook.
  • On a phone, the Home headline may wrap, About moves to the footer, and the story bar uses a lifebuoy button for “Need support?”.
  • The people here are 2D renders of the clay look. The 3D build uses the same joints, poses and colours. These are also the “Simple visuals” cut-outs.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts in these previews, so you need to be online. The build will self-host them.

One contradiction in the brief

Rule 1 says not to shorten the story, and section 3.6 says to keep every spoken line under about 6 seconds. Two lines may run longer (eA_05 and s4d_04). I took the safer option: the story stays as written and the two lines are flagged on the Motion and voice page.
