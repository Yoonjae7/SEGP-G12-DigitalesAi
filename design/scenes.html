Scene boards

One frame for each scene, S1 to S7, and both endings. Each is a stack of cut-paper layers with a depth number. Layers move at different speeds on a scene change (the paper-slide) and with the pointer or tilt.

On a phone the stage is portrait, so it shows the dashed column only. Every key moment sits inside it.

Light recipes

Each scene gets one recipe, so the whole story reads as one stage. Characters get the same tint as the scene, applied as a colour filter in 2D and as a light colour in 3D.

Motion between frames

  • Scene change is the paper-slide: layers move at different speeds, 420 ms. With reduced motion it is a 150 ms crossfade.
  • Camera moves (push-in, pan, sway) run slowly on the layer depths below. They stop when the learner turns on reduced motion.
  • Pointer parallax on desktop. Device tilt on mobile, only after the learner allows it. Never asked on load.
