A made-up example

What a “why” looks like

After two of the decisions, you write a few sentences. Then you get a short question that fits what you wrote.

This answer is made up. Yours can be shorter, longer or different.

Why 1

The photo is now in three chats. Who is responsible for what? Think about Lisa, the people who forwarded it, Sarah, and you. Write 2 to 4 sentences.

Sample answerLisa posted the photo and Marco and Dev passed it on. I should have said something sooner.
The story replies
Follow-upYou said who did what. Now add one thing: what could happen to Sarah because of it?

Every follow-up is written by the team. The reader only chooses which one to show.
